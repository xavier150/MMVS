# Macro Micro VR Service - Release Log
Release Logs: [GitHub Release Logs](https://github.com/xavier150/MMVS/wiki/Release-logs)


##  v0.4.7 Alpha (Talas Vore & Sound Design)
    - New sound design related to vore. (Planned.)
    - New: New notification sound when Player avoid getting spotted by Macro AI.
    - New: Desert level have now an ambient sounds.
    - New: New localized sounds for the Cozy House doors.
    - New: Ambient sounds in the pipes of Cozy House level where redo using soundscapes.
    - New: New sound for success and fail interactions feedback.
    - Fixed: Talas AI stop following branches and loop when want use a situation with the micro.
        (When Talas wants use a situation with micro he always try to move at the closer point from micro to see him, even when he is already close enough. 
        This make the AI task succeed and he stops following the next branches of the AI tree.)
    - Fixed: Many sound don't use the correct master class and are not affected by volume settings.
    - Fixed: Talas AI perception ingore micro distance and detect like if the micro is always front of him.
    - Fixed: Fade in/out menu is not visible when player respawn.
    - Fixed: Interaction on buttons always return fail sound when successful.
        
## Techs
    - Player AI feedback system:
        - All code related to player detection by Macro AI has been refactorised and moved to a dedicated module.

    - Sound Design:
        - All Sound Cue have been redo to use MetaSounds Sources.
        - Old sound class override in sound mix was replaced by Audio modulation and Sound Control Bus mix for the volume settings.
        - All AudioVolume actors were replaced by the new AudioGameplayVolume.
        - MMVS now use the Submix system to manage sound effects.
