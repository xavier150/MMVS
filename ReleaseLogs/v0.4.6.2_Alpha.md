# Macro Micro VR Service - Release Log
Release Logs: [GitHub Release Logs](https://github.com/xavier150/MMVS/wiki/Release-logs)


##  v0.4.6.2 Alpha (Talas Vore & Animations - Patch 2)
    - New: Talas Self Sight detection use real eyes position and radius instead of approximated point of view.
    - Change: When Macro AI can't reach target and move to see it, it will sometimes move at closer of the target to see it.
    - Fixed: Talas digestive system does not appear. (If you are swallowed, you will fall indefinitely into limbo.)
    - Fixed: Talas is ignoring his routine config and just keeps exploring.
    - Fixed: Talas can't see micro when using stand against wall situation.
    - Fixed: Talas sees the doors as walls when moving. 
        (Talas can interact with doors but in movement he don't detect the path to open and pass the door.)
    - Fixed: "Keep stuck and wear shoes" makes Talas walk spinning. 
        (Talas tries to walk towards the shoe while looking at the micro stuck to the sole. So he rotates on himself.))
    - Fixed: Talas AI can get stuck in endless loop when stop playing with micro stuck to sole. 
        (This is because it sets the branch as finished, starts the tree from the beginning and sets again that he wants to stop playing with the micro in an endless loop.)
    - Fixed: Cobble root animation don't follow VR headset position.
        (The body continue to face foward while the head rotate with the headset.)
    - Fixed: Control rotation is wrong on VR player users.
    - Fixed: VR hand scale don't follow player scale changes.
    - Fixed: VR player head and origin don't follow player scale changes.
    - Fixed: Cobble VR root animation offset is not applied locally.
        (Cobble body has an offset to stay behind the player headset but this offset is applied to the world origin instead of the local origin. 
        So when the player rescale the offset is wrong.)
    - Fixed: VR FX (teleportation preview and menu laser pointer) are not correctly scaled with player scale.
    - Fixed: Teleport fade menu never really close and affect performances.
    - Fixed: VR menu scale and position are not updated when player scale change.
    - Fixed: Scale computed for VR devices positions has one frame delay, and makes the camera shake with continuous rescaling.
        (Camera and hands use OpenXR world scale but the world scale update late, So the code will compare the calculated value and compensate the difference.)
    - Fixed: Player camera impulse when Talas steps close to them is never applied.
    - Fixed: When pinned the player is pushed under glass. (Wrong collision ray cast)
    - Fixed: Press between paws not always pin the player. 
        (Paw detection detect if the player is on the paw or under to pin or not. But when pushed the detection think the player is still on top of the paw.)
    - Fixed: Cobble is pushed to floor when pinned against a wall surface.
    - Fixed: Talas stops detecting Cobble in situation zones when he is pinned or stuck to Talas sole.
    - Fixed: Talas AI use wrong pathfinding module to found a way to grab micro and fail when the micro is too far.
        (This make Talas go away to try to grab Cobble when he can easly grab him.)
    - Fixed: When player respawn during a teleport, the teleport fade remain on screen.
